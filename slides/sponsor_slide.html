MCML
About

Munich Center Machine Learning is a joint LMU & TUM AI research center, one of six national AI Competence Centers in Germany, funded by the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology & Space and the Free State of Bavaria. Spans 11 research areas across three pillars: Foundations of ML, Domain-specific ML, and Perception, Vision & NLP, with a mission to develop responsible, trustworthy AI and support the next generation of researchers.

Advancing ML for medicine, biology & ethics through interdisciplinary research at the interface of computer science & cognitive science, supported by educational outreach to broaden ML and AI knowledge.
